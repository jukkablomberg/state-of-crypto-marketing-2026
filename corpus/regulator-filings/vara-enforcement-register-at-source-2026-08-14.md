## Why this file exists

`methodology.md` §3 names "VARA marketing-comms guidance" as a class-3 primary source. **VARA had never been swept at source.** It was named as *not reached* in six consecutive run records under watch (w) and watch (ee), while the class-3 register recorded **twelve consecutive zeroes** built entirely from search over EU-vocabulary secondary press.

The 2026-08-13 run record's recommendation 3 said, verbatim: *"Class 3: change the method, not the vocabulary."*

**The method change was executed once and it returned named, dated, primary-source marketing-side enforcement actions on the first fetch.** What follows is what the regulator publishes about itself.

## 2. Two findings the report can use

### 2.1 🔴 The corpus's "named marketing-side enforcement silence" was scoped wrong, and this file bounds it

Twelve consecutive run records recorded a class-3 zero and described it as *"day-N named marketing-side enforcement silence."* Read against this register, that phrasing was **too broad in scope and too strong in claim**:

- **In scope, it was only ever true of EU NCAs.** VARA published named, dated, fined marketing-perimeter actions throughout the window — including one dated inside 2026.
- **The silence that survives is narrower and still real:** *no EU national competent authority has published a named marketing-side enforcement action against a CASP since the MiCA transitional deadline.* That is the claim Phase 2 may print. The unqualified version may not.

**Watch (b) is unaffected on its own terms** — VARA is not an EU NCA and its most recent fine row predates the deadline by five and a half months. **What changed is the wording, not the verdict.** Every prior use of the phrase in this corpus should be read as EU-scoped.

### 2.2 🟢 A licensed-perimeter, marketing-regulations-only action against a named, significant protocol foundation

**The Open Network Foundation, 2025/07/24.** Category `Regulatory breaches`. Sole stated reason: **"Breaches of the VARA Marketing Regulations."** Sanctions: cease-and-desist, financial penalties, **and a Public Statement** — the only row in the register carrying a Public Statement.

This is the corpus's **first primary-source instance of a regulator fining a named, non-fringe crypto entity specifically and solely for how it marketed.** Every other marketing-limb row in the register is an unlicensed-operator perimeter case. It belongs to Theme 4 (exposure surface) and to Theme 1 (who owns the marketing gate), and it is the single most citable class-3 object the corpus has acquired.

## 3. Tracked-cohort exposure found in the register's neighbouring surface

The **regulatory-notices index** (surface 2) carries four 2026 Notices of Fines and three 2026 alerts that the fines table does not show. Two concern **tracked-cohort or cohort-adjacent exchanges**:

**KuCoin (Peken Global Limited) — TRACKED, Stratum 1.**
- **5 Mar 2026 — Investor and Marketplace Alert**, titled *"Phoenixfin Pte Ltd, MEK Global Limited, Peken Global Limited, and Kucoin Exchange EU Gmbh [commercially **advertising** as "Kucoin"]"*. Verbatim: *"Any promotion, advertising, or solicitation related to Kucoin has not been approved by VARA, and the company is therefore not allowed to offer, promote, or market any Virtual Asset products or services in Dubai or to its residents."* Also alleges the company *"may be … misrepresenting its licensing status"* — a marketing-comms allegation in substance.
- **24 Jun 2026 — Notice of Fines.** **Read honestly, the fine is a licensing action, not a marketing action:** the stated breach is *"providing Virtual Asset Broker-Dealer and/or Exchange Services to customers in Dubai without obtaining the necessary licence"*, cited against Federal Decree Law No. (10) of 2025, Dubai Law No. (4) of 2022 and Cabinet Resolution No. 111/2022. **Marketing is the prohibition limb of the March alert, not the charge in the June fine.** The notice also records that the entity *"fully cooperated"* and *"expressed its intention to enter VARA's licensing process"*, and narrows liability to Peken Global Limited alone, expressly excluding the other three named entities.

## 4. 🔴 Instrument caveat — VARA's two surfaces disagree, and a one-surface sweep under-counts

The fines table's most recent row is **2026/01/13**. The notices index carries **four Notices of Fines dated later than that** (24 Jun, 22 Jun ×2, 24 Jul 2026) which do **not** appear in the table.

**A sweep of `/enforcement/` alone would have concluded that VARA issued no fines after January 2026. That conclusion would be false, and the falsifier is one click away on the same domain.** Any future VARA sweep must read **both** surfaces. Recorded as a general lesson: *a regulator's summary table is not the regulator's record.*

Corollary for the counts in §1: the "0 rows after 2026-07-01" figure is a property of **the fines table**, not of VARA's enforcement activity.

## 5. Scope discipline — what this file does NOT establish

- It does **not** establish anything about EU NCA behaviour. VARA is not an NCA and MiCA does not apply in Dubai.
- It does **not** show a tracked-cohort firm being fined for marketing conduct. KuCoin's fine is a licensing charge; the marketing limb sits in the March alert.
- The 7 Oct 2025 "19 firms penalised" notice — *"for carrying out unlicensed Virtual Asset activities and for breaches of VARA's Marketing Regulations"*, fines **AED 100,000–600,000**, *"Unlicensed activity and unauthorised marketing will not be tolerated"* — **names no firms**. It is a numbered but **unnamed** action and cannot support any firm-level claim. Its 19 firms are not reconcilable to the table's rows from public information.
- Fine **amounts** are not published per row in the table. The AED 100k–600k range comes from the 7 Oct 2025 notice and applies to that action's 19 firms only. **Do not attach amounts to named rows.**
