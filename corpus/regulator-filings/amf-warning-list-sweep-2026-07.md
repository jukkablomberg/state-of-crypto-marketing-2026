## Why this sweep existed

Watch **(v)**, opened 2026-07-30: NCA *warning lists* had never been swept, in any jurisdiction. They are distinct from sanctions registers and much faster-moving. Unauthorised-CASP wind-down carries an explicit duty to *"cease marketing activities and solicitation"*, so if a first marketing-adjacent public action existed anywhere, a warning list was the most likely place it was already sitting. The prior run recorded that **the day-N silence finding was not fully safe until these were swept once.**

## The finding

**Three public warnings in two days (08–09 July), inside the post-deadline window, and not one of them is a marketing-conduct action.** Every captured item is an **unauthorised-entity / anti-fraud** warning: the wrongdoing alleged is *operating without authorisation*, not *advertising unfairly while authorised*.

This is the distinction the report must carry, because it is the distinction a reader will otherwise collapse:

> **The regulator's post-deadline output is perimeter enforcement, not conduct enforcement.** France has been publicly naming firms every week — for being outside the perimeter. It has named nobody for how they market inside it.

**Consequence for the headline null: it gets stronger, not weaker.** The null now survives a sweep of the instrument class most likely to falsify it. Phrasing that is now defensible:

> Through day 31 post-deadline, no EU NCA has taken a public, named, marketing-conduct action against an authorised CASP — in a window in which those same NCAs published multiple named warnings against unauthorised ones.

## Marketing-adjacent nuance worth keeping

The AMF's stated enforcement toolkit for unauthorised crypto services includes **public blacklisting and blocking access to the websites** — remedies that operate *directly on the marketing and distribution surface* rather than on the balance sheet. For Theme 4 this matters: the first-order regulatory risk to an unauthorised firm's marketing is not a fine, it is **erasure of the channel**.
