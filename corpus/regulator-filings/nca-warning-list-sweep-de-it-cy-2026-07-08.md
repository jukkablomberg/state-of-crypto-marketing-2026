Verbatim:

> "Die Finanzaufsicht Bafin warnt vor einer **Reihe nahezu identischer Websites**. Nach ihren Erkenntnissen bieten die Betreiber dort **ohne Erlaubnis** Bankgeschäfte und/oder Finanzdienstleistungen an. Die Betreiber der Websites werden nicht von der Bafin beaufsichtigt."

Legal basis stated: **§ 37 Absatz 4 Kreditwesengesetz (KWG).** Perimeter provision, not a promotions provision.

**Marketing-relevant detail worth keeping (Theme 1 / Theme 4).** BaFin's warning is **keyed on the marketing artefact itself** — the entities are grouped and identified by a shared *slogan* and a **near-identical site design** ("Verbessern Sie Ihr Krypto-Handelserlebnis" / "Secure. Fast. Effortless Crypto Management"). The regulator's detection unit here is **creative-and-template reuse across domains**, not corporate structure. That is a regulator reading a marketing surface as an identification signal, and it belongs in the report.

## Italy — CONSOB

**Primary captured:** `https://www.consob.it/web/area-pubblica/-/comunicazione-consob-del-2026-06-01-abusivismo` — fetched 2026-08-02, HTTP 200, dated **1 giugno 2026**.

Five sites blocked (mibinvestcfd.com, Omenyxgroup.com, Nexus Ai Group Limited, Pendoxa, Novate-cfd) under **delibere nn. 24010–24014 del 28 maggio 2026**, Art. 7-*octies* lett. b) TUF.

Verbatim, and this is the sentence that carries the sweep:

> "Sale, così, a **1723** il numero dei siti complessivamente oscurati dalla Consob a partire da luglio 2019… Di questi, **204** riguardano fenomeni legati a **cripto-attività**."

CONSOB names two distinct powers — the **"Decreto Crescita"** for unauthorised financial intermediaries, and, per its own framing elsewhere, **MiCAR + d.lgs. 129/2024** for blocking sites through which crypto-asset services are provided to Italian savers without authorisation.

**AI-fraud marketing note, captured first-party and directly relevant to Theme 4:**

> "è aumentato il ricorso a nuovi strumenti, come messaggi *e-mail* e siti *web* "clonati", **profili contraffatti di figure politiche, personaggi famosi e contenuti generati con sistemi di intelligenza artificiale - come immagini, voci o video** - con l'obiettivo di indurre i risparmiatori ad effettuare scelte di investimento dannose."

CONSOB links to a dedicated **`scheda_informativa_frodi_ai_esma_2026`** factsheet. **An NCA publishing standing guidance on AI-generated marketing creative as an investor-fraud vector** is a Theme-2/Theme-4 crossover the corpus did not previously hold.

## Cyprus — CySEC

**Primary captured (near-primary):** `https://cyprus-mail.com/2026/07/10/cyprus-regulator-warns-investors-regarding-unauthorised-crypto-asset-services` — fetched 2026-08-02, HTTP 200, `article:published_time` **2026-07-10T10:00:20Z**.

CySEC issued a formal reminder on **Friday 10 July 2026** that all crypto-asset services must comply with MiCA as of 1 July 2026, relaying an ESMA public statement.

**The operative sentence, and it is the most important thing in this file:**

> "Within the ESMA cooperation framework, **NCAs may, where necessary, take coordinated action against unauthorised CASPs after the transitional period**," the announcement concluded.

Also stated:

> "European and national competent authorities are **currently engaged directly with the relevant entities to ensure compliance**."

> These bodies "will coordinate to monitor whether **significant unauthorised cross-border service providers wind down without delay**, with a focus on client protection, financial stability and market integrity."

> Clients of unauthorised CASPs "do not benefit from MiCA safeguards, including protections for client assets"; clients are "invited to **act promptly**", including by transferring to an authorised provider or a self-hosted wallet.

**Why this is the strongest corroboration of the day-N null the corpus has obtained.** It is not the absence of evidence — it is **the regulators themselves describing enforcement in the prospective and conditional mood, ten days into the post-deadline window.** *"May, where necessary, take coordinated action."* *"Currently engaged directly."* *"Monitor whether… wind down."* Supervisory engagement, explicitly not yet enforcement, and explicitly scoped to **unauthorised** entities — i.e. perimeter — with no mention of marketing conduct by authorised firms.

**Related CySEC July output, noted not captured:** 2026-07-09, CySEC warned regulated firms to strengthen AML controls after the MiCA transition ended, citing new AMLA guidance (Cyprus Mail, 2026-07-09). AML, not marketing.

**Cross-reference worth flagging:** CySEC is also **Kraken's MiFID derivatives regulator** (Payward Europe Digital Solutions (CY) Ltd, licence no. 342/17), stated first-party on `kraken.com/europe-switch`. The NCA supervising one leg of a tracked firm's licence stack issued a perimeter reminder in the same window in which that firm's own campaign page lapsed. **Sequence, not causation, and no breach asserted** — but the report should not present NCAs as distant from the panel; they are inside its licence stacks.
