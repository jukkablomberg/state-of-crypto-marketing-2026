# CANONICAL — MARKETING TEAM SIZE AND BUDGET

**Built 2026-09-10 (Tier 0).** Jukka asked for a table of *company | marketing team size | marketing budget*.

🔴 **The honest headline: 1 of 27 tracked firms has a marketing team size. 0 of 27 has an in-window,
firm-stated marketing budget.** That is not a gap in the research — **it is the finding.** The most heavily
marketed consumer category of the decade does not disclose what it spends on marketing or how many people do it.

never yield a team size or a budget** without capturing posting body text.
