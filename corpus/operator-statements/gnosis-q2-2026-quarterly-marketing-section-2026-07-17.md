## 0. 🔴 Read this before citing: this file does not cleanly satisfy methodology §4

`methodology.md` §4 admits *"any public statement by a senior marketing operator (CMO / VP Marketing / Head of Brand / Head of Growth at a tracked firm)."* This document fails that test **twice**:

- **Gnosis is not a tracked firm.** It is perimeter (blockchain infrastructure; not in Stratum 1–4).
- **The Marketing section names no operator.** It is unattributed corporate text. Only the leadership statement carries a name, and that name is a co-founder, not a marketing officer.

**It is filed here anyway, labelled, because it is a source kind this corpus has not previously held and the report needs a name for it: firm-published periodic reporting on its own marketing function.** Not a job posting, not a podcast, not a press release, not an agency case study — a routine quarterly document in which a firm describes what its marketing team did and what tooling it used.

**Do not cite this as an operator statement.** Cite it as what it is, and let the fact that it does not fit be part of the finding: **§4's perimeter is drawn around individuals with titles, and it therefore cannot see a firm's own published account of its marketing function.** This is the same narrowness that has now cost the corpus twenty runs (watch (l)), arriving from a new direction. ## 1. ⭐ Verbatim — a firm states AI adoption inside its marketing and comms function

> *"We embedded AI across the marketing and comms function and saw this impact particularly our short form video (SFV) output and social media content improving our posting cadence while guarding quality."*

**This is Theme 2's missing evidence class.** Theme 2 is framed as *claimed adoption vs. JD-confirmed adoption* — a two-sided comparison between what firms say in interviews and what their job descriptions require. This is a third side: **what a firm writes down about itself, in a dated public document, when it is reporting to token holders rather than talking to press or recruiting.**

Note the specificity, which is what makes it usable. Not "we are AI-first." A named function (marketing and comms), a named affected output (short-form video and social content), a named metric direction (posting cadence up), and a named constraint (*"while guarding quality"*). **A firm reporting an AI-driven throughput gain and volunteering the quality caveat in the same clause is a more honest artifact than most of what this corpus's class-4 files contain.**

## 2. The rest of the Marketing section, verbatim and in full

> *"Our focus for the quarter was to support user acquisition and message testing for Gnosis App and to activate the brands at EthCC and later at Dappcon. We also managed communications around the exploit involving the delay module on Gnosis Pay and supported the launch of the MiniPay card."*

> *"Specific highlights include a solid improvements on cost-per-acquisition (CPA) for paid media for the consumer app, as we honed in on refined messaging and began to increase spend accordingly. … We continued to improve metrics and monitoring across the board. Our social presence outside of X grew, with multiple content pieces breaking the first 10k threshold on TikTok and solid performance particularly in YouTube."*

> *"We also improved transparency with the community by adding ecosystem sessions to our regular AMAs, and improving DAO documentation and forum UX."*

> *"In Q3 we will accelerate our progress in SEO / GEO, having implemented strong fundamentals this quarter and seeing solid initial performance. The focus for this will be Gnosis Pay where high quality content can support the generation of marketing-qualified-leads (MQL) and help put us in the consideration set for larger wallets and FinTechs."*

And from the Gnosis App product section:

> *"In parallel, we worked with the marketing team on the foundations for user acquisition across channels: the referral program, organic TikTok, paid Meta ads, and Circles Garage."*

### What is load-bearing here

- **"SEO / GEO"** — a firm naming *generative engine optimisation* as a named Q3 workstream in a routine quarterly document, not a thought-leadership post. Alongside §1, this is a firm treating AI as **both** a production input (SFV, social) **and** a distribution surface (GEO) in the same report.
- **A B2C-to-B2B marketing pivot, stated as a plan.** The named Q3 focus is MQL generation for Gnosis Pay aimed at *"larger wallets and FinTechs."* Read against §3 below — the consumer app being spun out, the team halved — this is a marketing function publicly reorienting from consumer acquisition to B2B demand generation. **Theme 1.**
- **Crisis comms named as marketing scope** — *"managed communications around the exploit."* Incident comms sitting inside the marketing report rather than a separate function is a gate-stack observation.
- **Named channel stack, dated:** paid Meta, organic TikTok, YouTube, referral, events (EthCC, DappCon), AMAs, SEO/GEO. **X is named only as the thing they grew *outside* of** — *"Our social presence outside of X grew."* For a crypto firm in 2026, publicly reporting growth measured as distance from X is worth one line in the report.
- ⚠ **CPA improvement is stated without a number.** *"a solid improvements on cost-per-acquisition"* — no baseline, no delta. **Do not print a CPA claim.** Record it as an unquantified self-report.

## 3. Context from the same document, needed to read §2 correctly

Leadership statement, **Friederike Ernst, Co-founder**, verbatim:

> *"On Gnosis App: growth has been linear, and linear is not good enough for a consumer product. … The consequence was a difficult one: we significantly reduced the team, and a focused group will bring a proposal to the DAO to fund Gnosis App independently. … These decisions were taken in the first week of July, after the quarter closed."*

And in the product section, on why the quarter underperformed:

> *"Two things slowed the quarter: the restructuring early in Q2, and the June Gnosis Pay service halt that left cards inactive for several days."*

**So the Marketing section above describes a quarter that contained a restructuring at its start, and was published seventeen days after a second, larger one.** The AI-embedding sentence in §1 is therefore not a statement made in calm conditions — it sits in the same document as a team reduction. **The corpus does not have evidence that these are causally linked and must not imply it.** What it has is the co-occurrence, dated, in one firm-published document — which is precisely the shape Theme 2 and Theme 5 meet at.

Headcount, from Kenk's reply of **20 July 2026, 3:37pm**, answering a governance question that asked for it directly:

**Team-scoped, not company-wide.** See tracker row 14 for the full adjudication.
