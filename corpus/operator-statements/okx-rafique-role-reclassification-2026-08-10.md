## 1. The role datum (Theme 1)

**Two captured sources describe the same named individual at the same Tier-1 tracked firm with two different functional scopes.** OKX's own estate says Chief Marketing Officer, overseeing marketing and brand growth. CoinDesk's in-window article says global managing partner for **Corporate Affairs and Investor Relations** — a function outside marketing entirely.

**WHAT IS CLAIMED:** as of the most recent in-window capture, the senior individual the corpus had recorded as OKX's Global CMO is publicly described, by a first-tier trade outlet in an interview that firm participated in, as a corporate-affairs and investor-relations executive.

**WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED, AND MUST NOT BE PRINTED:**

1. **Not a demotion, removal, departure or downgrade.** Nothing in either capture says the marketing responsibility ended. A title may have broadened rather than moved; publicly available profile summaries describe a combined "Global Managing Partner and CMO" scope spanning government and investor relations, marketing and brand partnerships. **That combined reading is at least as consistent with the evidence as a reclassification, and the corpus has not captured a primary for it.**
2. **No dated transition document exists in anything this corpus has captured.** There is no announcement, no press release, no dated org note. OKX's own CMO page has not been updated since **April 2024** — twenty-eight months.
3. The two sources are twenty months apart. Some drift is unremarkable. **What is remarkable is the direction and the silence.**

**THE ABSENCE IS THE FINDING, and it is a clean methodology-§-consistent one.** OKX is a MiCA-CASP-authorised Tier-1 exchange with an EU entity. The corpus can establish, from public sources, what its marketing seat was called in 2022. It cannot establish from any public source what that seat is called in 2026, who holds it, or whether the person it thought held it still does. **A regulator performing the same read would reach the same wall.** That is Theme 1 (gate-stack visibility) and Theme 4 (exposure surface) in a single artefact.

**Cross-reference, and it sharpens the point:** the corpus captured **two** dated 2026 first-party posts on OKX's own estate about the post-MiCA European surface this run — both authored by **Erald Ghoos, CEO of OKX Europe**, not by anyone with a marketing title. See `../marketing-campaigns/okx-europe-ghoos-licence-as-marketing-asset-2026-08.md`. **OKX's most prominent 2026 public marketing-adjacent authorship sits with a regional CEO; its named marketing executive appears in-window discussing legislation and investor relations.**
