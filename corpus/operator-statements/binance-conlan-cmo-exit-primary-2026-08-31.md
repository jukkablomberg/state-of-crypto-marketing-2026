## ⚠ Three cautions recorded at capture

**1. Three different tenure figures appear in one 2-minute article, and no two agree.**

- Conlan/the reporter: leaving *"after three years"* helping build the brand.
- The article's own sourced fact: she *"took the post in September 2023"* → **~2 years 9 months** to a 15 June 2026 exit.
- The Binance spokesperson: *"after four years of remarkable service."*

The most likely reconciliation is that the spokesperson counts total Binance service and the reporter
counts the CMO tenure — **but the article never says so, and this file asserts none of the three.**
Phase 2 may print *"took the CMO post in September 2023; last day 15 June 2026"*, which is the only
figure the primary states as a date rather than as a round number. Watch (vv).

Published **2026-05-12 11:25 ET**; `last_modified` **2026-05-19 10:18 ET**. Seven days, no changelog,
no update stamp in the body. Watch (ak) applies at full strength: **this capture is a snapshot of the
2026-08-31 state of the artifact, not of what was published on 2026-05-12.** The May 2026 essay that
relied on this scoop relied on a version of it nobody in this corpus ever captured. Anyone re-verifying

## Theme-1 signal, at the strength the primary supports

**Admissible:** *Binance's CMO seat turned over in May–June 2026. The publicly named interim occupant's
stated prior role is chief executive of a wallet business, not a marketing seat. The departing CMO
remains as an adviser. Binance's own public framing of the exit is personal ("to focus on personal
priorities"), not structural.*

**Not admissible from this source:** any claim about a search, about the interim brief's scope, about
Binance being first among Tier-1 firms to do this, or about the marketing org being restructured.

That narrower reading still carries Theme 1 — **an interim CMO drawn from a P&L seat rather than a
marketing seat is exactly the "marketing function reporting to a number" shape** Theme 1 tracks — and
it carries it with a citation, which the wider reading never had.
